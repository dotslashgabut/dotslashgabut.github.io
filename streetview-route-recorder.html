Recording needs desktop Chrome or Edge. On this device you can still load routes and preview each stop.

While recording, the stage button is hidden so it never appears in the video. Use M, C, S or Esc instead.

Tip: press F for fullscreen first, then R, to record at full screen size. Leaving fullscreen stops the recording and saves it.
